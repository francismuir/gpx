    Shortcuts & Editing Guide

    • New Track: Click map to add waypoints. Semi-transparent line connects points. Press Esc to finish.
    • Click Track (Explore Mode): Offer to insert a waypoint (entering Edit Mode) or split the track into two tracks at that point.
    • Click Track (Edit Mode): Automatically adds a waypoint at that point so you can drag it into position.
    • Move Waypoint: Drag any waypoint marker to a new position.
    • Extend Start/End: Click the first waypoint (WP0001) to prepend before, or the last waypoint to append after.
    • Delete Waypoint: Click a waypoint and choose Delete Waypoint.
